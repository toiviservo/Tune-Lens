How it works on real glasses

👂

1. Listen

The glasses mic array captures your instrument or voice, close to the source, with far less room noise than a phone across the room.

📈

2. Detect

On-device audio analysis finds the fundamental frequency with autocorrelation, then converts it to the nearest note and cents from the target.

🎯

3. Display

The HUD shows just the note, cents, and meter — large, high-contrast, and glanceable, so you never look away from playing.

⌖

4. Adjust via Neural Band

Fine-tune without touching the frames: a small wrist gesture or tap on the band switches mode, reference pitch, or mutes the HUD.

This prototype runs entirely in your browser. Your audio is analysed locally and is never uploaded. The HUD above is a 600×600 preview of what the same reading would look like in the glasses.
